1. Domain
- Feedback = Report do Resident gửi.
- Incident = sự vụ thực tế.
- Một Incident có thể chứa nhiều Feedback.
- Feedback không bị thay thế bởi Incident.

- Staff migration sang Incident đã gần hoàn tất.

5. Important Resident architecture

Resident Mobile should retain:

Feedback:
Resident's own submitted report.

Incident:
Public/community urban event.

DO NOT merge My Feedback into My Incidents.

Expected conceptual architecture:

Resident Mobile
├── Authentication
├── Home
├── Gửi phản ánh
├── Phản ánh của tôi
│   └── Feedback Detail
│       ├── Submitted information
│       ├── Public processing status
│       ├── Final resolution
│       ├── Rating
│       └── Private Staff conversation
├── Community
│   ├── Community Feed
│   ├── Community Map
│   └── Public Incident Detail
├── Area Alerts
├── AI Assistant
└── Profile / Notifications

6. Resident public Incident != Staff Incident Detail

Resident Public Incident may show:
- title
- public description
- category
- area/location
- map
- public status
- public processing progress
- public approved resolution/result

Resident must NOT receive internal Staff/Manager information such as:
- Staff internal workflow
- Provider Contact Log
- internal evidence
- duplicate/matching confidence
- Manager decision internals
- other Residents' private Feedback
- private reporter information
- SLA management controls
- internal NeedRework workflow

7. Status presentation

Do not expose internal workflow terminology directly if Resident does not need it.

Preferred public concepts:
- Đã gửi
- Đã tiếp nhận
- Đang xử lý
- Đang kiểm tra kết quả
- Hoàn thành
- Chờ bạn đánh giá

NeedRework and Manager approval mechanics are internal workflow details.

8. Privacy

If:
